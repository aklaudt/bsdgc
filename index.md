## About Us

Big Sioux Disc Golf Club (BSDGC) is a group of local players who love this sport and want to see it grow. Whether you've been throwing for years or just picked up your first disc, there's a spot for you here.

<div class="content-section">
  <h3>What We Do</h3>
  <p>We run regular events, help take care of local courses, and try to make it easy for anyone to get out and play.</p>
</div>
